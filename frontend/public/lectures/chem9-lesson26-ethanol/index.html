Molecular Architecture

Ethanol Molecule: The Reactive -OH Functional Group

Ethylic alcohol (Ethanol) has the molecular formula $\text{C}_2\text{H}_6\text{O}$ and condensed structural formula $\text{CH}_3-\text{CH}_2-\text{OH}$ (or $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$).

💡
Critical Structural Insight: Out of the 6 Hydrogen atoms in $\text{C}_2\text{H}_6\text{O}$, exactly ONE hydrogen atom is bonded to oxygen inside the $\mathbf{-OH}$ group. This hydrogen ($\text{H}^*$) is chemically active and replaceable by alkali metals like Sodium ($\text{Na}$). The other 5 hydrogens bonded to Carbon are inert to $\text{Na}$.

🔬 The Hydrogen Bonding Secret

The oxygen atom in the $-\text{OH}$ group is strongly electronegative, creating polar hydrogen bonds with neighboring water molecules ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$). This explains why ethanol is a liquid at room temperature and dissolves in water in all proportions, whereas ethane with the same number of carbons is a gas!

⭐ Medical Science Insight

Why is 70° alcohol more effective than 90° for sanitization?
At $90^\circ$, alcohol coagulates the outer bacterial protein shell too rapidly, forming a protective barrier that prevents alcohol from penetrating the bacterial core. At $70^\circ$, the presence of water slows down coagulation, allowing ethanol to thoroughly penetrate the cell membrane and destroy the microorganisms from within.

Station B • Functional Group Displacement

Active Metal Test: Sodium ($\text{Na}$) Reaction

When metallic sodium is introduced to absolute ethylic alcohol, the sodium sinks and steadily dissolves, producing hydrogen gas ($\text{H}_2$).

Na
Ready: Pure $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$ loaded
⚡ Single Displacement Equation
$$2\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH} + 2\text{Na} \to 2\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{ONa} + \text{H}_2\uparrow$$

Product: $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{ONa}$ is named Sodium ethoxide.
Scientific Law: The sodium atom displaces only the hydrogen atom in the $-\text{OH}$ group, demonstrating the chemical difference between hydroxyl hydrogen and hydrocarbon hydrogens.

Biohazard Protocol

Harmful Effects of Alcohol Abuse & Space Cadet Law

Ethanol is a central nervous system depressant. Abusing alcoholic beverages leads to severe physiological and societal damage.

🧠
Brain & Central Nervous System

Slows reaction times, impairs motor coordination, causes cognitive decline and loss of emotional control.

🫀
Cardiovascular Health

Elevates blood pressure, causes irregular cardiac rhythms, and increases risk of stroke.

🫁
Liver Pathology

Excessive ethanol processing produces toxic acetaldehyde, leading to fatty liver, hepatitis, and cirrhosis.
