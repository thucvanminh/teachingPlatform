Step-by-Step Telemetry

Standard 4-Step Protocol: Example Resolution

Step 1

Classify Reactivity & Write Balanced Chemical Equation

In the alloy of $\text{Fe}$ and $\text{Cu}$, only $\text{Fe}$ stands before Hydrogen in the activity series:

$$\text{Fe} + 2\text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{FeCl}_2 + \text{H}_2\uparrow$$

$\text{Cu}$ sits after Hydrogen $\implies$ zero reaction, remains as unreacted solid residue.

Step 2

Compute Gas Moles from Volume Telemetry

$$n_{\text{H}_2} = \frac{V_{\text{H}_2}}{24.79} = \frac{2.479}{24.79} = 0.1\text{ (mol)}$$
Step 3

Stoichiometric Bridge to Reacted Metal Mass

By stoichiometric ratio $1 : 1$, $n_{\text{Fe}} = n_{\text{H}_2} = 0.1\text{ mol}$:

$$m_{\text{Fe}} = n_{\text{Fe}} \times M_{\text{Fe}} = 0.1 \times 56 = 5.6\text{ (g)}$$
Step 4

Compute Remaining Inert Metal & Mass Percentages

$$m_{\text{Cu}} = m_{\text{mix}} - m_{\text{Fe}} = 12.0 - 5.6 = 6.4\text{ (g)}$$
$$\%m_{\text{Fe}} = \frac{5.6}{12.0} \times 100\% = 46.67\%, \quad \%m_{\text{Cu}} = 100\% - 46.67\% = 53.33\%$$

Mission Objective: Passing $7.437\text{ L}$ (std conditions) mixture of $\text{CH}_4$ and $\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$ through excess $\text{Br}_2$ solution. The flask mass increases by $2.8\text{ g}$. Determine the mass and volume percentages of each gas.
1
Identify Absorbed Component: $$\text{C}_2\text{H}_4 + \text{Br}_2 \rightarrow \text{C}_2\text{H}_4\text{Br}_2$$ $$m_{\text{C}_2\text{H}_4} = \Delta m_{\text{flask}} = 2.8\text{ (g)}$$ $$n_{\text{C}_2\text{H}_4} = \frac{2.8}{28} = 0.1\text{ (mol)}$$
2
Determine Unreacted Methane ($\text{CH}_4$): $$n_{\text{mix}} = \frac{7.437}{24.79} = 0.3\text{ (mol)}$$ $$n_{\text{CH}_4} = n_{\text{mix}} - n_{\text{C}_2\text{H}_4} = 0.3 - 0.1 = 0.2\text{ (mol)}$$ $$m_{\text{CH}_4} = 0.2 \times 16 = 3.2\text{ (g)}$$
3
Mass Percentages ($\%m$): $$m_{\text{mix}} = 2.8 + 3.2 = 6.0\text{ (g)}$$ $$\%m_{\text{C}_2\text{H}_4} = \frac{2.8}{6.0} \times 100\% = 46.67\%, \quad \%m_{\text{CH}_4} = 53.33\%$$
4

For gases, volume fraction strictly equals mole fraction:

$$\%V_{\text{C}_2\text{H}_4} = \frac{0.1}{0.3} \times 100\% = 33.33\%, \quad \%V_{\text{CH}_4} = 66.67\%$$
