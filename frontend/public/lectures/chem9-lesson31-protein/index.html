SGK Concept Summary

What Is a Protein? (KHTN 9 Core Facts)

Essential structural facts for Lesson 31 (SGK pp. 138–139).

📘
Definition: Proteins are complex organic compounds with very large molecular masses, composed of many amino acid units linked together by peptide bonds.
Feature Key Telemetry
Building Blocks Many amino acid units (e.g., alanine, glycine)
Connecting Bond Peptide bond ($-\text{CO}-\text{NH}-$)
Molecular Mass Very large: tens of thousands to millions of amu
Natural Occurrence Found in both animals and plants (not only animals!)
Why Proteins Differ Different sequences / arrangements of amino acids give different structures and functions
🛰️
Amino Acid Comparison: Alanine ($\text{H}_2\text{N}-\text{CH}(\text{CH}_3)-\text{COOH}$) and glycine ($\text{H}_2\text{N}-\text{CH}_2-\text{COOH}$) share $-\text{NH}_2$ and $-\text{COOH}$ groups, differing only in their side group ($-\text{CH}_3$ vs. $-\text{H}$).

Chemical Properties I & II

Coagulation & Thermal Decomposition

Proteins change state easily under chemical agents and heat.

🧊
1. Coagulation (Clumping / Solidifying):

Proteins change properties and coagulate under the action of:

  • Acids (e.g., adding $\text{HCl}$ to egg white or souring milk)
  • Bases (alkaline solutions)
  • Heat / Temperature (e.g., boiling or frying an egg)
🔥
2. Decomposition on Strong Heating (No Water):

When heated strongly at high temperature without water, protein decomposes or chars, releasing volatile gases and a characteristic burnt odor (like burning hair).

🍳
Everyday Connection: Cooking meat, fish, or eggs uses heat-induced coagulation of protein to make food firm and digestible.

Human Physiology

Key Biological Roles of Protein in the Body

Proteins have diverse structures tailored to four major roles in living organisms.

🏗️ Structural Role

Builds cells and tissues such as muscles, skin, and hair.

⚡ Catalytic Role

Enzymes are specialized proteins that catalyze biochemical reactions.

📡 Hormonal Role

Many hormones are proteins that regulate physiological processes.

🩸 Transport Role

Carries nutrients and oxygen (e.g., hemoglobin transports $\text{O}_2$ to cells).

⚠️
Common Exam Traps (SBT KHTN 9):
  • Providing primary energy is NOT protein's main role (carbohydrates & lipids do that).
  • Storing genetic information is NOT protein's role (DNA does that).
  • Not all foods contain protein, and protein exists in both animals and plants.
