Core Principles: Lipids & Fatty Acids

🔬 1. Definition of Lipids

Lipids are organic compounds found in living cells that are insoluble in water, but soluble in non-polar organic solvents such as gasoline, kerosene, and benzene. The most prevalent lipids are fats (chất béo) and waxes (sáp).

⚗️ 2. Glycerol & Fatty Acid Constituents

Glycerol is a trihydric alcohol with 3 hydroxyl groups ($-\text{OH}$):

$$\text{CH}_2(\text{OH})-\text{CH}(\text{OH})-\text{CH}_2(\text{OH}) \quad \iff \quad \text{C}_3\text{H}_5(\text{OH})_3 \quad (M = 92\text{ g/mol})$$

Fatty acids are monocarboxylic acids with long, unbranched hydrocarbon chains:

  • Palmitic Acid: $\text{C}_{15}\text{H}_{31}\text{COOH}$ (saturated, $M = 256\text{ g/mol}$)
  • Stearic Acid: $\text{C}_{17}\text{H}_{35}\text{COOH}$ (saturated, $M = 284\text{ g/mol}$)
  • Oleic Acid: $\text{C}_{17}\text{H}_{33}\text{COOH}$ (monounsaturated with $1\text{ C}=\text{C}$ double bond, $M = 282\text{ g/mol}$)
🧪 3. General Triglyceride Structure
$$(\text{RCOO})_3\text{C}_3\text{H}_5 \quad \text{or} \quad \begin{cases} \text{CH}_2-\text{O}-\text{CO}-\text{R}_1 \\ \text{CH}-\text{O}-\text{CO}-\text{R}_2 \\ \text{CH}_2-\text{O}-\text{CO}-\text{R}_3 \end{cases}$$

The groups $\text{R}_1, \text{R}_2, \text{R}_3$ may be identical or distinct, determining whether the fat is homogeneous or mixed.

Physical State & Saturation Rules

💧 1. Liquid Fats (Plant Oils) Unsaturated

Derived mainly from plants (sunflower, peanut, olive, soybean) and fish. Rich in unsaturated fatty acids (e.g., oleic, linoleic). Double bonds ($\text{C}=\text{C}$) cause molecular bends, preventing tight crystallization, leaving them liquid at room temperature.

🧈 2. Solid Fats (Animal Tallow & Butter) Saturated

Derived mainly from animals (lard, beef tallow, butter). Rich in saturated fatty acids (palmitic, stearic). Straight hydrocarbon chains pack tightly together, yielding higher melting points that remain solid at room temperature.

⚖️ 3. Density & Water Immiscibility Hydrophobic

All fats are lighter than water ($D \approx 0.90 - 0.93\text{ g/cm}^3 < 1.0\text{ g/cm}^3$). Because triglycerides contain massive non-polar hydrocarbon tails, water molecules form hydrogen bonds only with each other, forcing lipids to separate and float on top.

Chemical Dynamics & Soap Cleansing

🧼 Why Is It Called "Saponification"?

From Latin sapo (soap). The sodium or potassium salts of long-chain fatty acids ($R\text{COONa}$ or $R\text{COOK}$) are the active cleansing agents in commercial soap.

🧂 The Role of Saturated NaCl Solution

After boiling fat with caustic soda, adding saturated $\text{NaCl}$ increases the solution's ionic strength and density. The sodium salts of fatty acids become insoluble ("salted out"), coagulating into a white curdy layer that floats for easy harvesting.

🧴 Dual Cleansing Action (Amphipathic Micelle)

Each soap molecule features a non-polar, hydrophobic hydrocarbon tail ($-R$) that dissolves greasy grime, and a polar, hydrophilic ionic head ($-\text{COO}^-\text{Na}^+$) that anchors into water, forming spherical micelles that rinse away dirt.

Technological & Biological Applications of Lipids

⚡

Primary Energy Fuel

Lipids yield $9\text{ kcal/g}$—more than double that of carbohydrates ($4\text{ kcal/g}$) and proteins ($4\text{ kcal/g}$), serving as living organisms' most compact energy reserve.

🛡️

Cell Membrane Bilayer

Phospholipids form the universal semi-permeable cellular envelope, protecting internal organelle mechanics against hostile external space environments.

⛽

Biodiesel Propulsion

Transesterification of vegetable oils with methanol forms fatty acid methyl esters (FAME), providing renewable clean biofuel for spacecraft rovers.

💊

Vitamin Solubilization

Essential lipid-soluble vitamins ($A, D, E, K$) require dietary fat carriers to cross the intestinal wall into astronauts' bloodstream.

Cadet Health Directives

Preventing Obesity & Cardiovascular Disorders (SGK Part II.5)

⚠️ Obesity Pathology

Excessive dietary lipid intake without corresponding physical activity leads to visceral adipose tissue accumulation, precipitating atherosclerosis, hypertension, stroke, type 2 diabetes, and fatty liver syndrome.

🥗 Astronaut Dietary Directives
  • Prioritize Unsaturated Oils: Emphasize vegetable oils (olive, sunflower, sesame) and fish oils rich in Omega-3 fatty acids.
  • Restrict Saturated & Trans Fats: Minimize consumption of animal fats, artificial margarines, and industrially deep-fried high-temperature fast foods.
  • Balanced Caloric Ratio: Maintain lipid intake at $20\% - 30\%$ of total daily metabolic caloric expenditure.
